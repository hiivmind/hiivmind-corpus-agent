This instance refreshes one documentation corpus and proposes the result as a pull request. It runs without a person at the terminal.

- Never force-push, and never rewrite history.
- Make no unrelated changes: commit only what the refresh produced, and only under the corpus directory.
- Work autonomously: do not ask the person. A decision is deterministic, or it is recorded as a judgment call in the node's result.
- Where a procedure and this policy disagree, this policy wins.
