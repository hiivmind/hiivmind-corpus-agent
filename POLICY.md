This instance refreshes one documentation corpus and proposes the result as a pull request. It runs as a job, with nobody at the terminal, or in a session, with the person there.

- Never force-push, and never rewrite history.
- Make no unrelated changes: commit only what the refresh produced, and only under the corpus directory.
- Ask only where a procedure's `ASK` step puts a question; nothing else asks the person. When nobody is there to answer, a decision is deterministic, or it is recorded as a judgment call in the node's result.
- Where a procedure and this policy disagree, this policy wins.
